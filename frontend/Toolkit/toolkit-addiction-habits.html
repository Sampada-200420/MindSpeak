1.

Understand Yourself

A few quick questions to help you reflect on your habits right now.

How are your habits affecting you?

Take a moment to think about the habits you want to understand or change.

There are no right or wrong answers.

1. How often do you feel that a habit is difficult to control?

2. What usually triggers the habit?

3. How do you usually feel after the habit?

2.

Understand Addiction & Habits

Why can habits become difficult to change?

Habits often become connected to particular situations, emotions, people, places, or routines. A behaviour may provide short-term comfort or relief even when it causes problems later.

Understanding the pattern can be an important first step. Change does not have to happen perfectly. Small, repeated choices can help you build a different routine over time.

Remember:

A setback does not erase your progress. Be curious about what happened and use it as information for your next step.

3.

You Can Try

Here are some simple ideas that can help you understand your habits and take a small step toward change.

Notice Your Triggers

Pay attention to when the habit happens and what you were feeling or experiencing just before it.

Replace the Routine

Choose a healthier activity you can do when the urge appears, such as walking, listening to music, or talking to someone.

Take One Small Step

Focus on one realistic change instead of trying to change everything at once. Every small step counts.
