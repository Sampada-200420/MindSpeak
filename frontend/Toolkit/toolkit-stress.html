2.

Understand Stress & Burnout

What are stress and burnout, and why do they happen?

Stress is your body and mind’s response when you feel pressured, challenged, or overwhelmed. A little stress can help you respond to situations, but too much stress for too long can leave you feeling drained.

Burnout can develop when ongoing demands leave you physically or emotionally exhausted. You may feel tired, detached, less motivated, or like even simple tasks require too much energy.

Remember:

You don’t have to be productive every moment. Taking a pause, setting boundaries, and asking for support can be meaningful steps toward feeling better.

3.

You Can Try

Here are some simple ideas that can help you create a little more space for yourself.

Take a Real Pause

Step away from your work or studies for a few minutes. Stretch, breathe, drink some water, and let your mind reset.

Make It Smaller

Choose just one important task instead of trying to handle everything at once. Small progress still counts.

Give Yourself Recovery Time

Protect some time for sleep, food, movement, hobbies, or simply doing nothing. Recovery is part of taking care of yourself.

Connect with Friends and Mentors

You don’t have to carry everything alone. Reach out, share, and find support.
