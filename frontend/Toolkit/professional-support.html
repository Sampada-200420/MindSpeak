YOUR PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

Four Ways to Contribute

Learn how professionals can connect their expertise with the MindSpeak community.

01
SHARE YOUR EXPERTISE

Professional Involvement

Bring your professional background and knowledge to the MindSpeak community in ways that are appropriate to your role and expertise.

  • Share relevant knowledge
  • Contribute educational resources
  • Support community learning
  • Encourage responsible help-seeking
02
BUILD TRUST

Application & Verification

Professional participation begins with providing relevant information and completing the required verification process.

  • Provide professional details
  • Submit supporting information
  • Complete verification
  • Maintain accurate information
03
HELP PEOPLE LEARN

Community Contribution

Use your experience to contribute useful, accessible and responsible information to people within the MindSpeak community.

  • Create helpful resources
  • Participate in community activities
  • Share practical knowledge
  • Promote supportive conversations
04
BE PART OF THE COMMUNITY

MindSpeak Participation

Connect with MindSpeak initiatives and contribute to a community that values kindness, respect, safety and responsible support.

  • Join relevant initiatives
  • Participate in events
  • Collaborate with community members
  • Support a culture of wellbeing
PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY

Expertise comes with responsibility.

Professional contribution should always respect appropriate boundaries, privacy, safety and the limits of your professional role.

Stay within your area of expertise.
Respect privacy and confidentiality.
Communicate information responsibly.
Refer people to appropriate services when needed.
