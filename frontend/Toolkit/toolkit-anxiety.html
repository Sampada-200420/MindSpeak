2.

Understand Anxiety

What is anxiety and why does it happen?

Anxiety is your body’s natural response to stress or uncertainty. It can show up as racing thoughts, restlessness, a fast heartbeat, or a feeling that something bad might happen.

It often happens when we’re facing challenges, change, or situations that feel uncertain. A certain amount of anxiety is normal, but it can become difficult when it starts affecting your everyday life.

Remember:

Anxiety doesn’t mean something is wrong with you. You’re not alone, and things can feel more manageable with the right support.

3.

You Can Try

Here are some simple ideas that can help you feel calmer and more in control.

Try a Breathing Exercise

Take a few slow, deep breaths. Inhale for 4, hold for 4, and exhale for 4. Repeat a few times and notice how you feel.

Write It Out

Write down what’s worrying you. Seeing your thoughts on paper can help you understand them and feel less overwhelmed.

Take a Small Step

Do one small thing that helps you feel grounded, like going for a short walk, listening to music, or talking to someone you trust.

Connect with Friends and Mentors

You don’t have to go through this alone. Reach out, share, and find support.
