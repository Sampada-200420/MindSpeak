YOUR MENTOR JOURNEY

Three Steps to Becoming a Mentor

Build your knowledge, practice your skills, and learn how to make a positive impact.

1
BUILD YOUR KNOWLEDGE

Learn

Understand the foundations of mental health support and learn practical ways to communicate with care.

What you'll learn

  • Mental health basics
  • Active listening
  • Supportive communication
  • Boundaries and ethics
  • Safety and referral guidance
2
BUILD YOUR CONFIDENCE

Practice

Apply what you learn through realistic situations, interactive activities, reflection and guided practice.

Practice through

  • Real-world scenarios
  • Guided conversations
  • Self-reflection
  • Difficult situations
  • Feedback and tips
3
SUPPORT YOUR COMMUNITY

Make an Impact

Use your skills to become a safe, supportive and responsible presence within the MindSpeak community.

As a mentor, you can

  • Be there for others
  • Encourage help-seeking
  • Promote kindness and respect
  • Respect boundaries
  • Help create a safer community
YOUR ROLE

A mentor doesn't need to have every answer.

Good mentoring is about listening, showing empathy, respecting boundaries and helping people find appropriate support when they need it.

Listen before trying to solve.
Respect each person's experience.
Know your limits and responsibilities.
Know when to refer someone for extra help.
MAKE A DIFFERENCE

Together, we can create a kinder community.

Every thoughtful conversation matters. Start learning today and grow into a mentor who helps others feel heard, respected and supported.
