2.

Understand Low Mood

What can cause low mood?

Everyone has periods when they feel down, tired, unmotivated, or less interested in things they normally enjoy. Difficult experiences, stress, loneliness, changes in life, or simply feeling overwhelmed can all affect your mood.

Sometimes there is no obvious reason for feeling low. Your feelings are still valid. Paying attention to your mood and giving yourself care can be an important first step.

Remember:

A low mood does not define who you are. You can take things one day, or even one small step, at a time. Reaching out for support is okay.

3.

You Can Try

Small actions can be easier than trying to change everything at once.

Take One Small Step

Pick one simple thing you can do today, such as taking a shower, going outside, making food, or tidying one small space.

Do Something Gentle

Listen to music, watch something comforting, sit outside, draw, or spend a few minutes doing something you enjoy.

Reach Out

Send a simple message to someone you trust. You don’t need to explain everything. Even saying “I’m having a difficult day” is enough.

Connect with Friends and Mentors

You don’t have to go through difficult days alone. Reach out and find support.
