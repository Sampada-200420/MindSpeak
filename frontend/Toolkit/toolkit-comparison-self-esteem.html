2.

Understand Comparison & Self-Esteem

Why do we compare ourselves?

Comparing yourself to others is a normal human tendency. It can sometimes motivate us, but it can also lead to self-doubt, stress, and lower self-esteem, especially when we only see someone else’s highlights and not their full story.

Remember, everyone’s journey is different, and you are more than a single moment, achievement, or social media post.

Remember:

You’re not behind. You’re on your own path. Your worth isn’t measured by comparing, but by being yourself.

3.

You Can Try

Here are some simple ideas that can help you feel more confident and kinder to yourself.

Notice & Pause

When you catch yourself comparing, take a moment to pause. Remind yourself that you’re seeing a small part of someone else’s story.

Focus on Your Strengths

Write down three things you like about yourself or are proud of. They can be big or small — they all matter.

Be Kind to Your Inner Voice

Challenge negative thoughts and replace them with kind, realistic ones. Speak to yourself like you would to a good friend.
