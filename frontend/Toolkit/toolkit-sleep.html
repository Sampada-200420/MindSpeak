2.

Understand Sleep

What is sleep and why is it important?

Sleep is your body’s natural way to rest, recover, and recharge. It helps your brain process information, supports your mood, strengthens your memory, and keeps your body healthy.

Sometimes sleep can be affected by stress, screen time, irregular routines, or a busy mind. The good news is — better sleep is possible, and small changes can make a real difference.

Remember:

It’s okay to have difficult nights. Everyone struggles with sleep sometimes. What matters is being kind to yourself and taking small steps forward.

3.

You Can Try

Here are some simple ideas that can help you get better sleep.

Create a Wind-Down Routine

Try doing calming activities before bed, like reading, stretching, or listening to gentle music. Give yourself some screen-free time.

Make Your Sleep Space Comfortable

Keep your room cool, quiet, and dark. A comfortable environment can help your body relax and fall asleep more easily.

Clear Your Mind

If your mind is busy, try writing down your thoughts or tomorrow’s to-do list before bed. This can help you worry less at night.
